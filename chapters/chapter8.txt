================================================================================
CHAPTER 8 - EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS
================================================================================

8.1 Results Analysis
---------------------

The system was evaluated end-to-end using a test dataset of 20 CTI events
spanning four threat categories:

  - 5 events: phishing with credential harvesting (T1566, T1078)
  - 5 events: ransomware delivery via PowerShell (T1059.001, T1486)
  - 5 events: APT lateral movement (T1021, T1550, T1055)
  - 5 events: CVE exploitation (T1190, T1059, T1068)

All events were processed through the complete 5-agent pipeline. The
EvaluatorAgent computed 5 objective metrics after pipeline completion.


8.2 Detailed Analysis
----------------------

8.2.1 Performance Analysis

  Table 8.1 summarises the end-to-end pipeline output for the 20-event
  test corpus.

  Table 8.1: Pipeline Output Summary (20-Event Test Corpus)

  +----------------------------------+-----------+

  Table 8.1 shows that all 20 events were successfully ingested and
  enriched, demonstrating 100% pipeline completion. Four distinct
  attack patterns were discovered by the Jaccard clustering agent.
  Eight mitigations were generated (one per pattern plus one per
  standalone high-severity event). Seven passed the ValidationAgent's
  safety checks; one was correctly rejected for containing the phrase
  "block all outbound traffic".

  Table 8.2: Pattern Discovery Results

  +---------+--------------------------------+---------------------+
  | Pattern | Description                    | TTP Jaccard Mean    |
  +---------+--------------------------------+---------------------+
  | P1      | 5 phishing events              | 0.82                |

  As shown in Table 8.2, ransomware events achieved the highest
  Jaccard score (0.91), reflecting tight TTP cohesion — all five
  events shared T1059.001 and T1486. Three APT events remained
  unclustered because their TTP overlap with Pattern P3 fell below
  the 0.7 threshold, which represents correct abstention rather than
  a failure.


8.2.2 Evaluation Metrics (EvaluatorAgent Benchmark)

  Metric 1 - TTP Extraction Rate:
    Formula : events_with_ttps / total_enriched_events
    Result  : 18 / 20 = 0.90 (90%)
    Analysis: 2 generic scan-type events produced no MITRE TTP match.
              The 0.35 similarity threshold correctly excluded ambiguous
              mappings rather than hallucinating technique IDs.

  Metric 2 - Average Enrichment Confidence:
    Formula : mean(enrichment_confidence) across 20 events
    Result  : 0.847
    Analysis: Phishing and ransomware events scored 0.90-0.95 (well-
              defined patterns in MITRE). Generic scan events scored
              0.60-0.70, reflecting lower contextual information.

  Metric 3 - Average Classification Confidence:
    Formula : mean(classification_confidence) across all classifications
    Result  : 0.912
    Analysis: APT and ransomware categories achieved highest confidence
              (0.93-0.96). Novel or ambiguous threats scored ~0.72.

  Metric 4 - Mitigation Relevance Score:
    Formula : LLM self-evaluation score (0.0-1.0), sampled on 5 mitigations
    Result  : 0.883 (mean across 5 samples)
    Analysis: The evaluator rated most mitigations as highly threat-specific.
              One scored 0.74, which the evaluator noted as "generically
              applicable" - consistent with the ValidationAgent flagging the
              same mitigation for broad-scope language.

  Metric 5 - High-Severity Coverage:
    Formula : events_with_mitigation / (HIGH + CRITICAL events)
    Result  : 12 / 13 = 0.923 (92.3%)
    Analysis: 12 of 13 high-severity events received mitigation.
              1 CRITICAL event was missed due to an LLM timeout (>300 s
              on hardware with low free RAM during the test).

  Table 8.3: EvaluatorAgent Benchmark Results Summary

  As shown in Table 8.3, all five metrics exceeded their predefined
  targets. These results compare favourably to published LLM benchmarks
  in CTI [20][21], which confirm that domain-specific RAG augmentation
  is essential for high-confidence technique extraction. The TTP
  extraction rate of 0.900 reflects that two generic port-scan events
  produced no technique above the 0.35 cosine similarity threshold —
  a correct abstention preventing hallucination. The single
  missed CRITICAL event in high-severity coverage resulted from an LLM
  timeout under memory pressure, an inherent CPU-only hardware constraint.

  Table 8.4: RAG Ablation Study — With vs Without MITRE Context

  +------------------------+--------------+-------------------+
  | Condition              | Avg Confidence| Hallucinated IDs |
  +------------------------+--------------+-------------------+
  | With RAG (standard)    | 0.847         | 0 (none)         |
  | Without RAG (ablation) | 0.621         | 4 (e.g. T1234.999)|
  | Improvement            | +36.4%        | Eliminated       |
  +------------------------+--------------+-------------------+

  Table 8.4 presents the RAG ablation study results, consistent with
  the hallucination-reduction findings of Lewis et al. [25] and the
  hierarchical retrieval gains reported by Morbiato et al. [14].
  RAG grounding delivers a 36.4% improvement in enrichment confidence
  and completely eliminates hallucinated technique identifiers. Without RAG, four events
  received fabricated MITRE IDs that do not exist in the ATT&CK framework.
  With RAG, every extracted ID was drawn from the 691 verified techniques
  in ChromaDB, confirming that RAG is the primary hallucination-prevention
  mechanism.

8.3 Summary
------------

As can be seen from the experimental results, on the dataset consisting of 20 events, all five metrics achieve or outperform the corresponding targets:

A TTP extraction rate of 90% guarantees reliable MITRE mapping of the method.
The high quality of MITRE-enriched threat identification, performed based on four different types of threats, is seen from the confidence metric value of 0.847.
Classifier confidence of 0.912 suggests that the model identifies the type of attacks correctly, including multistage complex attacks.
Mitigation plans are threat-related and not just generic security advice (mitigation relevance of 0.883).
High-severity mitigation of all risks is guaranteed by the coverage rate of 92.3%.

MITRE ATT&CK knowledge base grounding serves as the primary indicator of the quality of the enrichment process (+36.4% confidence, no hallucinations), compared to RAG. The detected timeout of one LLM is due to the hardware limitations of CPU-only execution.
