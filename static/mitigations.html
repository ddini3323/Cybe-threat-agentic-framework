4 Mitigation & Validation Agents

Steps 4 and 5. The Mitigation Agent generates response plans using the LLM, then the Validation Agent safety-checks each recommendation before it can be approved.
Step 4 — Mitigation Agent: The LLM generates actionable mitigation recommendations from two sources:
• Pattern-based — mitigations for discovered threat patterns (groups of related events)
• Event-based — mitigations for individual high-severity events (severity ≥ 3)

Each mitigation includes a title, description, step-by-step action plan, and optionally a sample security rule (firewall/SIEM/IDS).

Step 5 — Validation Agent: Before mitigations reach an analyst, they are automatically checked for:
• Dangerous commands — e.g., rm -rf /, DROP DATABASE
• Overly broad scope — e.g., "block all traffic", "disable all services"
• Completeness — at least 2 actionable steps with sufficient detail

In plain terms: Step 4 is the AI writing an incident response playbook. Step 5 is a safety reviewer making sure the playbook won't accidentally cause more damage than the threat itself. Only then can a human analyst approve it.
